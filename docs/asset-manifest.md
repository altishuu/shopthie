# Shopthie Phase 1 — real-product asset manifest

Reviewed: **October 27, 2026**. Research evidence only; not production inventory.

## Outcome and recommended art direction

- **8 downloaded photographs / 7 exact-model catalog entries / 4 categories / 5 brands.** Counts are computed from the companion JSON, not a requested target.
- Recommended hero: `public/images/products/hero-apple-airpods-max.jpg` — **1920 × 1277 JPEG**, a real photograph of AirPods Max on a reflective black surface. Use in a graphite cinematic section, with off-white typography in a separate text column. This is **not transparent**; do not treat it as a cutout or apply `mix-blend-multiply` on off-white.
- Secondary hero/card option: `apple-airpods-max-detail.jpg` (1599 × 1600). The numbers “2” and “3” in the Commons source filenames are **photo indices**, not AirPods generations. Do not call these “AirPods Max 2/3” or label them USB-C.
- Use neutral off-white product panels and generous whitespace rather than imitating a manufacturer campaign. Preserve authentic wood/gray/white photographic backgrounds; the catalog is coherent editorial photography, not a uniform packshot set.
- Licensed photography, not press-room assets: all selected files are marked photographer **Own work** on Commons; no manufacturer image-reuse assumption was used. Exact page/license evidence appears below.
- Older photographed products (notably QuietComfort 25, Flip 3 and PLAY:1) are intentional Phase 1 seed evidence, **not a claim that these are current new-stock products**. Any frontend price or availability must be explicitly demo.

## Verification and limitations

- Each selected source file page was fetched directly, not inferred from a search snippet; Commons ImageInfo API supplied creator/license/original-size metadata. The direct license deeds were also fetched successfully.
- All eight decoded downloads and all eight final assets passed Pillow image verification; all local files are true progressive JPEG bytes, not HTML responses with image extensions. SHA-256 values and exact byte counts are in `docs/catalog-sources.json`.
- All eight photos were visually reviewed (hero/secondary individually; the remaining six as a contact sheet). Exact variants depend on photographer metadata, not silhouette-only inference. No storage, connector, capacity, current stock, or performance claim is supplied.
- Originals on `upload.wikimedia.org` returned HTTP 429. Rather than save error pages, the task used the **1920-pixel Commons thumbnail variants returned by the live ImageInfo API** on `thumb.wikimedia.org`; all succeeded and decoded. Original URLs are documented for provenance, not misrepresented as the downloaded variant.
- Local transformations only: EXIF orientation normalization where present, proportional downsizing without cropping, RGB JPEG re-encoding (quality 91, progressive). No generative fill, replacement product, background removal/replacement, brand mark removal or product retouching.
- Exact written product evidence is in `docs/catalog-sources.json`. The hero is a dark real-product photo, not a transparent PNG; a rights-verified transparent cutout was not produced.
- Rejected alternatives included display-store iPhone images with hands/security fixtures, Samsung group shots with uncertain isolation, cluttered Sonos One photographs, and screen-facing Pixel images that introduce wallpaper rights. Only the selected exact-model images were installed.
